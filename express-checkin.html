Let's get you out the door in twenty minutes.

Do the paperwork here, from your phone, before you drive over. When you show up we hand you keys — not a clipboard.

You'll need about 6 minutes and your phone camera.
  • Driver's license (front and back)

Driver's license

Lay it flat, good light, all four corners in frame. This is what the state requires us to have on file before you can drive off.

Address check

Does the address printed on your license match the address you just gave us — the one above?

No problem — we'll just need two proofs of residence. Because you've moved, the lender needs two documents showing your name at the new address. We'll collect those on the next screen.

Two proofs of residence

Your license still shows the old address, so we need two documents that show your name and your new address, dated in the last 30 days.

Any two of these work:
  • Bank or credit-union statement
  • Cell phone bill
  • Utility bill — electric, gas, water, internet
  • Current lease or mortgage statement
  • Insurance statement or vehicle registration

A screenshot of the online version counts, as long as your name, the address and the date are all visible. Two documents from the same company usually won't be accepted — mix them up.

Insurance

Indiana law says the car can't leave the lot without coverage. Send us the card now and we'll have your agent squared away before you arrive.
