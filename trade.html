Getting started

Fill the box with the barcode

Driver's door jamb sticker, or the barcode on your registration. Get close — the barcode should run nearly wall to wall. Sideways is fine.

Not catching it? Get closer until the barcode nearly fills the box, and tap 🔦 if the jamb is dark. Still nothing — use Snap a photo instead, or Cancel and type the VIN in.
